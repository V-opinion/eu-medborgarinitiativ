The New Paradigm

We must adopt a revolutionary framework built upon MRVS (Multi-Religious Value System), MRV (Mutual Recognition and Validation), and Multi-Religion Support.

1. MRV: Mutual Recognition and Validation

  • For Israelis: Validating the Palestinian narrative of the Nakba and their right to self-determination.
  • For Palestinians: Recognizing the Jewish people’s ancient, unbroken historical and spiritual connection to the land.

MRV outlaws the political weaponization of denial in educational curricula.

Extracting the shared ethical cores of Judaism (Tikkun Olam), Islam (Salam), and Christianity to form a constitutional "Council of Abrahamic Values." Resource allocation (like water) is governed by shared religious imperatives, not military might.

3. Multi-Religion Support

Establishes an Interfaith Peacebuilding Corps. When tensions rise over holy sites, political leaders step back, and deployed Rabbis, Imams, and Clerics manage de-escalation.
