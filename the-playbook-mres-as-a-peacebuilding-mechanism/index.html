Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions about the MultireligionValSystem MRES project.

What is a multi-religion election system?

A multi-religion election system replaces church election with modern religious democracy for fair representation.

How does it work?

All faith communities participate in elections, ensuring equal dignity and peaceful governance.

Why replace church election?

Church election only serves Protestant faith. Multi-religion system includes Jews, Muslims, Buddhists, Hindus, and others.
